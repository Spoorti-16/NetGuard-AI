AI-Driven Network Intrusion Detection, Fault Diagnosis and Automated Response

Mininet generates normal, attack and fault traffic. Wireshark/tshark captures it. Machine-learning models and protocol rules decide which attack is happening or which fault (DNS, refused, timeout, 404, 5xx, packet loss) is breaking the service, then the system scores the threat and can block the source.
